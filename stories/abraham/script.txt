Why would God ask a father...
to sacrifice his own son?
It's one of the hardest stories in the Bible.
Abraham waited twenty-five years for a son.
Isaac was the promise.
And then God says:
"Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac, whom thou lovest."
For three days, Abraham walks toward the mountain.
Isaac carries the wood on his back.
Then Isaac asks:
"Father...
where is the lamb?"
And Abraham answers:
"God will provide himself a lamb."
At the top, Abraham raises the knife...
and God stops him.
Caught in a thicket nearby —
a ram.
So why the test?
In that world, gods demanded children.
Abraham's neighbors did it.
But this God stopped the knife.
He was showing Abraham — and every generation after — that He is not that kind of God.
Abraham named the place:
"The Lord will provide."
But here's what most people miss.
Centuries later, on that same mountain range, another Father would give his only Son.
Another Son would carry wood on his back.
And this time... no one stopped it.
Because the lamb Abraham promised...
was always coming.
